# Stage 5 · Text

Order of writing: figures and legends → Results → Summary/Abstract → Introduction → Discussion → Methods → title, highlights, eTOC.

- **Results**: each section headed by a declarative sentence; opens with the question, ends with the conclusion and the bridge to the next. Numbers with n, unit and test. Past tense for what was done, present for what it shows.
- **Summary/Abstract**: context (1 sentence) · gap (1) · what was done (1–2) · findings with the key numbers (2–3) · meaning (1). No citations, no undefined abbreviations.
- **Introduction**: problem · what is known (from the field map's consensus) · the gap (from its open questions) · what this paper does. Cite the turning-point papers, not reviews.
- **Discussion**: what changes · agreement and disagreement with named papers, with the likely reason · limitations (Cell: its own section) · what follows.
- **Claims match evidence**: motif ≠ binding; co-location ≠ interaction; association ≠ cause; in silico ≠ perturbation. "First" needs a field scan that found no earlier report.
- Limits: `jf count main.md --journal <id>`.
- Cell specifics: `vendor/cell-skills/skills/{cell-summary,cell-highlights,cell-framing,cell-writing,cell-star-methods}`; Nature: `vendor/nature-skills/nature-writing`, `nature-polishing`.
- STAR Methods order: Key resources table · Experimental model and study participant details · Method details · Quantification and statistical analysis; plus Resource availability (lead contact, materials, data and code).
- Declare AI assistance as the journal requires; authors remain responsible for every sentence and reference.
