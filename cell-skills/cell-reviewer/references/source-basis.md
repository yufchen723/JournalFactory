## What does not come from Cell

cell.md contains **no** text on:

- instructions to referees or a referee report form;
- how editors triage, how many referees are used, or decision categories;
- criteria for returning a paper without review;
- confidentiality or AI-use rules for referees;
- presubmission inquiries, transfers or appeals.

For any of these: "not specified in cell.md — check cell.com".

Everything in this skill about referee behaviour, typical objections and editor triage is **inference / craft guidance**: general peer-review practice applied to the criteria in cell.md §2. Label it that way in every output.

## Source hierarchy

1. The manuscript and notes the user supplies.
2. cell.md, for rules and the quoted criteria.
3. Craft guidance in this skill, marked as such.

## Use of nature-skills

The structure (stance, bounded review, concern ledger with pointers, major/minor split, source-basis file) is adapted from nature-reviewer. Nature's assessment axes and policies are not imported. Do not describe a Cell review with Nature's criteria.

## Limits of a simulated review

- It reads only what is supplied. Missing figures or methods bound the review.
- It cannot check raw data or images.
- It does not know the current literature beyond what the user supplies or what is verified through `cell-citation`. A novelty judgment needs a literature check.
- It is not a prediction of the decision.
