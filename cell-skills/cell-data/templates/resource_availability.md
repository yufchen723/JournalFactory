# Resource availability — template

> **Cell rule (cell.md §3):** this section is required and has exactly these three subsections. It sits after
> the Discussion and before Acknowledgments. One lead contact, who must be a corresponding author.
>
> **The sentences below are patterns, not Cell's required wording.** The exact wording is on the
> journal-policies page of cell.com, which was not read. Open that page and align the text before submission.
>
> Replace every [bracket]. Keep one option per bullet and delete the others. Delete this note.
> Never write an identifier that does not yet exist.

## RESOURCE AVAILABILITY

### Lead contact

Requests for further information and resources should be directed to and will be fulfilled by the lead contact, [Full Name] ([e-mail]).

### Materials availability

*Choose what applies; one statement per kind of material.*

- This study did not generate new unique reagents.
- [Plasmids / cell lines / strains] generated in this study have been deposited at [repository] ([identifiers]).
- [Materials] generated in this study are available from the lead contact with a completed materials transfer agreement.
- There are restrictions on the availability of [material] because [reason]. [What can be shared, and how.]

### Data and code availability

*Address data, code and any additional information, each explicitly.*

**Data**

- [Data type, e.g. sequencing] data have been deposited at [repository] and are publicly available as of the date of publication. Accession numbers are listed in the key resources table.
- [Human data type] data have been deposited at [controlled-access repository] under accession [ID]. Because [consent / legal reason], access requires approval by [data access committee]; requests are made through [route].
- [Imaging / other] data have been deposited at [repository] ([DOI or accession]).
- This paper analyzes existing, publicly available data. The accession numbers for these datasets are listed in the key resources table.
- This paper does not report [standardized datatype] data.

**Code**

- All original code has been deposited at [archive] and is publicly available as of the date of publication. The DOI is listed in the key resources table.
- This paper does not report original code.

**Additional information**

- Any additional information required to reanalyze the data reported in this paper is available from the lead contact upon request.
